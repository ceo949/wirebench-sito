2. What data we process

Data you send us through the quote request form:

  • first and last name, email address, phone number (optional), country;
  • make, model and year of the vehicle and a description of the problem;
  • the photos of the wiring harness you upload;
  • confirmation that you have read this policy.

The photos are used to assess the harness. Please do not include people, number plates, chassis numbers (VIN) or documents unless they are needed for the assessment. Photos taken with a phone may contain hidden data (for example the date and GPS location of the shot): if you do not want to share it, turn off location in your camera or remove it before sending.

Data you send us by writing to us (email, phone): whatever you choose to give us.

Browsing data: when you visit the site, the hosting service (GitHub Pages) automatically logs technical data such as your IP address, browser type and requested pages, for security and to run the service. The site does not use profiling cookies or analytics tools.

We do not ask for special categories of data (health, opinions, etc.): please do not enter them in the form.

8. Your rights

Under Articles 15–22 GDPR you may at any time ask for:

  • access to your data (Art. 15);
  • correction of inaccurate or incomplete data (Art. 16);
  • erasure of your data, photos included (Art. 17);
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18);
  • data portability (Art. 20);
  • objection to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21);
  • withdrawal of any consent given (Art. 7(3)), without affecting processing carried out before.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority, Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it), or with the supervisory authority of the EU country where you live or work.

Write to the contacts in section 1: we reply within one month (Art. 12 GDPR).

9. How we protect your data

We process data electronically and, where needed, on paper, only for the stated purposes, with appropriate technical and organisational measures: encrypted connection (HTTPS), access limited to authorised staff, backups and a data breach procedure.

10. Automated decisions

We do not take automated decisions or carry out profiling that has legal effects on you (Art. 22 GDPR). Quotes are prepared by a person.
