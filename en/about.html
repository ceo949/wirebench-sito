About us

WireBench is the brand through which Nuova Elettronica S.r.l. brings its trade out of the workshop: making wiring harnesses.

Nuova Elettronica is based in Avigliana, near Turin, Italy, and has been making wiring harnesses since 1978. It is an ISO 9001 certified company.

Two things come from here:

  • WireBench Lab, the lab that repairs and remakes to measure the wiring harnesses of classic cars and motorcycles, especially where no replacement exists.
  • WireBench — The Harness Maker's Handbook, the practical guide that puts in writing how a harness is made and checked.

How we work

At the bench, one harness at a time. We have crimping tools for almost every terminal on the market. Every job starts from the customer's photos and ends with the harness checked against the sample. We do the harness only: we do not touch the vehicle's electrical system.

Nuova Elettronica has been making wiring harnesses since 1978: we build, repair and restore them for companies and private customers. Today we work from our premises in Avigliana (Turin), in the Susa Valley, and we are an ISO 9001 certified company.
