WireBench Lab

Classic wiring harness repairs and replicas

When the harness of a classic car or motorcycle is burnt, cut or patched up, and no replacement exists, there are two ways forward:

  • Repair: we fix your harness, replacing wires, terminals and protection where needed.
  • Replica from your sample: we build a new harness by copying your original: lengths, branches, wire colours, terminals.

We work on the wiring harness only: we do not remove or fit the electrical system on the vehicle. We have crimping tools for almost every terminal on the market; wires and braided sleeving are bought for each job.

How it works, step by step

  1. You send us photos

    Fill in the quote request with your vehicle details and photos of the harness. We look at the photos and tell you whether the job can be done. This first check is free.

  2. You ship us the part

    If the job can be done, you ship us the harness. Shipping is at your expense.

  3. We inspect the harness — €40 + VAT

    With the part on our bench we check everything the photos cannot show: condition of wires, terminals, connectors, missing parts. The inspection costs €40 + VAT and is always charged: if you then order the job, it is one line of the final invoice; if you do not, you receive an invoice for €40 only.

  4. You receive the quote — valid for 15 days

    The quote states the work, materials, lead time and price. It is valid for 15 days from its date. If during the work we find problems that could not be seen at inspection, we send you an updated quote before going on.

  5. You confirm the order — 50% deposit

    You pay 50% when ordering. Payment by bank transfer or PayPal.

  6. We work on the harness

    We buy any missing material and do the job at the bench. At the end we check it against the sample.

  7. Balance and return shipping

    You pay the balance before shipping and we send the harness back to you. Return shipping is also at your expense.

If you decide not to go ahead, the harness is returned to you, with shipping at your expense.

Indicative lead times

  • Simple harness, with material already in the workshop: shipped back the day after we receive it.
  • More complex harnesses, or material to be ordered: lead time depends on the job and on the supplier; it is stated in the quote.

Prices

We do not publish a price list: every harness is different. The price depends on labour hours and materials, and you find it in the quote after the inspection. The only fixed costs are those written above: first photo check free, inspection of the part €40 + VAT.

Shipping and customs

Outbound and return shipping, customs, duties and import taxes are paid by the customer. VAT treatment is stated in the quote.

Materials

If your job needs a special material sold only in a minimum batch (for example a wire in a colour no longer in catalogue), the quote includes the whole batch and the leftover material is shipped to you with the job.

Warranty

12 months on our workmanship, excluding damage from incorrect fitting or improper use. Your statutory consumer rights are not affected.

Invoice

Invoices are issued by Nuova Elettronica S.r.l.
