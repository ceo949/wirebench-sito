Frequently asked questions

Repairs and replicas

Which vehicles do you work on?

Mostly Italian classic cars and motorcycles, including rare models for which no harness is on the market. For other vehicles, send us photos: we will tell you whether we can do it.

Do you also redo the vehicle's electrical system?

No. We work on the harness only: we do not remove it from or fit it to the vehicle, and we do not diagnose the electrical system.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the harness: labour hours and materials. That is why we publish no price list and quote case by case. The first check on your photos is free; inspecting the harness you ship to us costs €40 + VAT.

Why do I pay for the inspection even if I don't order?

Because it is real work: opening up the protection, checking wires and terminals, working out what is needed. If you order the job, the €40 is one line of the final invoice; if you don't, you receive an invoice for €40 only.

How long is the quote valid?

15 days from its date.

What if you find more problems during the work?

We send you an updated quote before going on. We do no extra work without your confirmation.

How long does it take?

A simple harness, with material already in the workshop, is shipped back the day after we receive it. For more complex jobs or material to be ordered, the lead time is written in the quote.

How do I pay?

50% when ordering, balance before shipping. Bank transfer or PayPal. Invoices are issued by Nuova Elettronica S.r.l.

Who pays for shipping?

The customer: both ways, plus customs, duties and import taxes when shipping from outside Italy.

Do you work with customers outside Italy?

Yes, with shipping and customs paid by the customer. VAT treatment is stated in the quote.

What warranty do you give?

12 months on our workmanship, excluding damage from incorrect fitting or improper use. Your statutory consumer rights are not affected.

Will the new harness be the same as the original?

We remake it by copying your original: lengths, branches, colours and terminals.

What if a material is sold only in batches?

If a special material sold in a minimum batch is needed, the quote includes the whole batch and the leftover material is shipped to you with the job.

What if I decide not to go ahead?

You pay the inspection of the part (€40 + VAT) and we ship the harness back to you, with shipping at your expense.

Can I see past work?

Yes, on the Past work page and on the YouTube channel @wirebenchlab, as jobs are delivered.

The handbook

When is the handbook out?

When it is ready, with all its photos. There are no pre-orders.

How much is it and how do I get it?

We will announce the price at release to everyone who asked for information on the handbook page. It will be a downloadable PDF.

Which language?

The Italian edition comes first. The English edition is in preparation.

Can I pass it to a colleague?

No: each copy carries the buyer's email and is for personal use. Copying or sharing it is forbidden.
